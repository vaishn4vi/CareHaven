About CareHaven

Simplifying everyday healthcare, one tool at a time.

Our Story

CareHaven started as a simple idea among four friends who wanted to address a common problem in everyday healthcare: how fragmented it is across different apps and services. We wanted a single, calm place where people could track personal health metrics, find nearby hospitals, manage appointments, and learn from trustworthy information — without switching between a dozen apps.

We combined our different strengths to build something genuinely useful, focused on making healthcare more accessible, organized and efficient for everyone who uses it.
